# Slope Studio

Unlock YouTube monetization by operating Slope Studio through a role-based Paperclip company.

## Loop

1. Growth Lead selects the next growth/SEO bet.
2. Screenwriter writes the script package.
3. QA / Critic returns `PASS` or `FAIL` on the script.
4. Producer creates visuals, audio, final video, metadata, publish execution, and journal link after script PASS.
5. QA / Critic returns `PASS` or `FAIL` on the final video before publishing.
6. Analytics & Learning measures performance and extracts lessons after maturation.
7. Observability / Ops checks workflow health, cost drift, missed routines, and incidents.
8. Secretary reports daily progress to Telegram.

## Settings

Day-to-day control should happen through native Paperclip tasks and comments,
not by editing this package after import.

Project settings docs:

- `settings/company-settings.md` - where settings live and how to change them.
- `settings/channels.md` - YouTube channel list and channel switching.
- `settings/autopilot-policy.md` - budgets, videos per day, cadence, approval policy.
- `settings/seo-policy.md` - SEO, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, packaging.
- `settings/paperclip-control.md` - how to steer the company from the Paperclip UI.
