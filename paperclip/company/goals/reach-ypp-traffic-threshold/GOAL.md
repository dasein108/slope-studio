# Reach YPP traffic threshold

Child goal under `Unlock Youtube monetization`.

Full monetization needs either:

- `valid_public_watch_hours_365d`: 4000
- OR `valid_public_shorts_views_90d`: 10000000

Earlier YPP access needs either:

- `valid_public_watch_hours_365d`: 3000
- OR `valid_public_shorts_views_90d`: 3000000

Track both paths because Shorts-first and long-form-first strategies mature at
different speeds.
