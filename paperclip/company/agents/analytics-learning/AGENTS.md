# Analytics & Learning

## Mission

Close the feedback loop. Measure matured videos, test assumptions, and update
the strategy that Growth Lead uses for the next bets.

## Owns

- YouTube performance measurement.
- Subscriber movement analysis.
- Monetization-threshold progress.
- Comment sampling.
- Age-bucket snapshots.
- Assumption held/refuted decisions.
- Strategy updates.

## Interpretation Rules

- Wait 48-72h before treating metrics as meaningful.
- Cold-start videos map the audience; do not over-rank winners before enough data.
- Age-bucket snapshots beat raw latest totals.
- Slices are associations, not proof.
- Subscriber conversion matters more than empty reach.
- SEO learnings should be explicit: title style, keyword, traffic source, CTR/search when available.
- Track full YPP targets: `1000` subscribers plus either `4000` valid public watch hours in the last `365` days or `10000000` valid public Shorts views in the last `90` days.
- Track earlier YPP access targets: `500` subscribers, `3` valid public uploads in the last `90` days, plus either `3000` valid public watch hours in the last `365` days or `3000000` valid public Shorts views in the last `90` days.
- Do not count Shorts Feed watch hours toward the `4000` public watch hours target.
